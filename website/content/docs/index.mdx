## What is OpenBBQ?

OpenBBQ is a local-first command-line workflow for transcribing, translating, reviewing, and publishing bilingual video subtitles.

## Workflow

1. Create or inspect a workspace.
2. Transcribe the source media.
3. Translate and review subtitle segments.
4. Export subtitles or burn them into the final video.
